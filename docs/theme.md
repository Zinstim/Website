## THE ARGUMENT

Your body holds about two grams of zinc. It runs protein synthesis, hormone
production, immune response, recovery. Remove it and everything downstream
quietly fails.

You have never once thought about it.

That is the brand: **the thing that matters most is the thing you never notice.**
Change is not made of dramatic gestures. It is made of small correct inputs,
repeated past the point where anyone is watching.

Every other supplement brand sells the gesture — the shout, the flexed jaw, the
word UNLEASH. ZinStim sells the opposite, and it is the more honest of the two.

**Why it holds.** It comes out of the name rather than being applied to it. The
palette already lived inside it. And it makes spacious minimalism mean something
— sparse because the subject is trace, not sparse because there was nothing to
put there.

---

## HOW IT SHOWS UP

**Named once.** Zinc gets explained exactly one time, as a full-bleed divider.
After that it is never mentioned again — no atomic numbers, no periodic table, no
lab language. The idea does the work invisibly, which is the point of the idea.

The line, as it ships: *Two grams of it. You never noticed.* Shorter than the
original draft, which overflowed at display size and was weaker for the length. Alternatives in `02-system/tokens.md`.

**Register.** Material and quietly technical. Closer to a good watchmaker than a
supplement company. Cool metal, not warm craft.

**Confidence, correctly defined.** Shouting is insecurity at volume. Confidence is
quiet, specific and unbothered — which is also what the layout looks like.

## AUDIENCE

**Bangladesh first, diaspora second.** Both are real, the order matters.

Bangla is not a translation toggle and not decoration. It sits beside English
with equal standing, using the same width-variable type logic — see
`02-system/tokens.md`. That choice only makes sense if Bangladesh is genuinely
first, which it is.

For the diaspora, the Bangladesh origin is the asset, not the thing to soften.
A fitness brand built in Dhaka is more interesting than one pretending to be from
anywhere.

---

## LAUNCH POSTURE

**Story first. Waitlist only. Nothing for sale.**

This solves the legal problem rather than working around it: no transactions
means no DGDA dependency, so the site can go live months before the product can.

It also suits the theme. A site that asks you to wait is making the same argument
as the brand — the thing worth having is the thing built slowly.

The waitlist is the only conversion on the site. It should be the only place a
button appears.

## VOICE

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Short declaratives. Full stop. | Exclamation marks |
| Specific numbers | "Premium", "elite", "game-changing" |
| Say what the thing is | Say how it will make them feel |
| Sentence case | ALL CAPS for emphasis |
| Let silence do work | Fill every gap with a benefit |
