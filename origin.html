Origin

Where the name comes from, what we refuse to do, and why any of this is happening in Dhaka.

The name is not a metaphor.

Zinc is a trace element. You are carrying about two grams of it right now, spread across every tissue in your body. It runs protein synthesis. It runs hormone production. It runs immune response and wound repair.

Remove it and everything downstream fails, quietly, over months. You would not be able to point at the cause.

That is the whole idea behind this company. The thing that matters most is usually the thing nobody notices.

Every brand here sells the gesture.

The shout. The flexed jaw. The word UNLEASH in capital letters. It works because it is legible, and it fails because nobody remembers which brand they just looked at.

The people who actually change are the ones doing small correct things long after anyone stopped watching. That is less dramatic and far harder, which is exactly why nobody sells it.

Why Dhaka.

Because it is where we are from, and because the market here deserves better than it currently gets.

Counterfeits are the single biggest thing Bangladeshi customers worry about when they buy a supplement, and they are right to worry. The honest answer is not a louder claim. It is publishing the numbers.

01

Every batch goes to an independent lab before it goes anywhere near a tub.

02

Full panel published. Numeric heavy metals, not the word "complies".

03

The batch number on your tub matches a report you can read on this site.

What we refuse to do.

  • Blends If we will not tell you the dose, there is a reason, and it is never a good one.
  • Before and after Photographs of other people's bodies are not evidence of anything.
  • Urgency No countdown timers, no fake scarcity. It will still be here tomorrow.
  • Untestable claims If a lab cannot measure it, we do not print it.
